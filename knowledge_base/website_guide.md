# NTU Pool Website Guide / 网站使用指南

---

## Account / 账号

---

### Q: How do I register an account?

**A:** Open the **Register** page, fill in a username, email, and a password of at least 8 characters, then enter the 6-digit verification code (OTP) sent to your email. The code expires in 10 minutes; you can resend it after a 60-second cooldown.
中文：打开注册页，填写用户名、邮箱和至少 8 位的密码，然后输入发送到邮箱的 6 位验证码完成验证。验证码 10 分钟内有效，60 秒后可以重新发送。

---

### Q: Why should I not use my NTU email to register?

**A:** NTU mailboxes (@ntu.edu.sg / @e.ntu.edu.sg) currently block or filter the verification email, so the 6-digit code may never arrive. Please register with a personal mailbox such as Gmail or Outlook instead. If you already registered with an NTU email and cannot receive the code, register again with a personal email.
中文：NTU 校园邮箱目前会拦截验证邮件，导致收不到 6 位验证码。请改用 Gmail、Outlook 等个人邮箱注册。

---

### Q: I did not receive the verification code. What can I do?

**A:** First check the spam/junk folder. Make sure you did not use an NTU email address. On the verification page you can click resend after the 60-second cooldown. After 5 wrong attempts the code is locked for 15 minutes.
中文：先检查垃圾邮件文件夹，确认没有使用 NTU 邮箱。验证页面 60 秒冷却后可重发验证码；连续输错 5 次会锁定 15 分钟。

---

### Q: How do I reset my password?

**A:** Use "Forgot password" on the login page. Enter your email, receive a 6-digit reset code, then set a new password (minimum 8 characters).
中文：在登录页点击"忘记密码"，输入邮箱收取 6 位验证码，然后设置新密码（至少 8 位）。

---

## Live Status & Reports / 实时状态与上报

---

### Q: How can I submit a manual pool report?

**A:** Log in with a verified account, then use the **Report Status** button next to the status card on the homepage and choose "Pool is Open" or "Pool is Closed". Guests can view reports but cannot submit them.
中文：使用已验证的账号登录后，在首页状态卡旁点击 **Report Status** 按钮，选择"泳池开放"或"泳池关闭"。游客只能查看、不能上报。

---

### Q: Who is allowed to submit a manual report?

**A:** Only logged-in users whose email is verified can submit reports. This keeps the crowdsourced signal spam-free.
中文：只有完成邮箱验证并登录的用户可以上报，以防止刷报。

---

### Q: How many reports are needed to override weather status?

**A:** The community consensus rule needs the **latest 5 reports from 5 different verified human users within a 30-minute window, all agreeing**, and the newest of them must be within 10 minutes. Reports from the site's community bot accounts are excluded from consensus. When consensus forms, it overrides the weather-based status.
中文：共识规则需要 30 分钟内来自 5 位不同真人用户的最近 5 条上报完全一致，且最新一条在 10 分钟内。社区氛围账号的上报不计入共识。共识形成后会覆盖天气判定。

---

### Q: How long do reports stay visible on the homepage?

**A:** The homepage shows the latest 10 reports from the past **24 hours**. Reports older than 2 hours are visually dimmed; anything older than 24 hours is hidden so stale reports cannot be mistaken for the current situation.
中文：首页展示过去 **24 小时**内最新的 10 条上报。超过 2 小时的会变灰，超过 24 小时的不再显示，避免误导。

## Community / 社区

---

### Q: How do I search for posts in the community?

**A:** Use the search box at the top of the Community page. It matches keywords in post titles and content (both English and Chinese), and can be combined with the category tabs (General, Find Buddy, Lost & Found, Tutorials).
中文：社区页顶部有搜索框，支持按标题和正文关键词搜索（中英文均可），并可与分类标签组合使用。

---

### Q: How do I create a post?

**A:** Log in with a verified account, open the Community page, and press **New Post**. Choose a category, write a title and content, and optionally attach one JPEG/PNG image up to 2 MB.
中文：使用已验证账号登录后，在社区页点击 **New Post**，选择分类、填写标题和正文，可选配一张不超过 2MB 的 JPEG/PNG 图片。

---

### Q: How do comments, likes, and saves work?

**A:** Verified users can comment on posts (including nested replies), like posts and comments, and save posts to their profile collection. Post authors and admins can delete their own content; deletion is a soft delete.
中文：已验证用户可以评论（支持楼中楼）、点赞帖子和评论、收藏帖子。作者和管理员可以删除内容（软删除）。

---

### Q: How do I report inappropriate content?

**A:** Every post and comment has a Report option where you describe the reason. Each user can report a given item once; admins review reports in a dedicated dashboard.
中文：每个帖子和评论都有举报入口，填写原因即可。同一内容每人只能举报一次，管理员会在后台处理。

---

### Q: How do I change my nickname or upload an avatar?

**A:** Open **Profile → Edit profile**. You can set a display nickname and upload an avatar image (JPEG or PNG, up to 2 MB). The nickname is what other users see next to your posts, comments, and pool reports; your username stays fixed.
中文：进入 **Profile → 编辑资料**，可以修改昵称并上传头像（JPEG/PNG，不超过 2MB）。昵称是别人在你的帖子、评论和上报旁看到的名字，用户名本身不变。

---

### Q: How do I delete my own post or comment?

**A:** Open your post (or find your comment) and use the Delete option. Only the author and site admins can delete a given item. Deletion is a soft delete: the entry disappears from the feed, and a deleted comment that still has replies is kept as a placeholder so the thread stays readable.
中文：打开自己的帖子或找到自己的评论，点击删除。只有作者本人和管理员可以删除。删除为软删除：内容从列表中消失；如果被删的评论下面还有回复，会保留一个占位以免楼层断裂。

---

### Q: Where can I see the posts I saved?

**A:** Saved (collected) posts appear on your **Profile** page, alongside the list of posts you have written. Use the save/collect action on any post to add it there.
中文：收藏过的帖子会显示在你的 **Profile 个人主页**，和你自己发过的帖子列在一起。在任意帖子上点收藏即可加入。

---

### Q: How do I filter posts by category?

**A:** The Community page has category tabs: All, General, Find Buddy (squad), Lost & Found, and Tutorials. Selecting a tab filters the feed, and the filter combines with the search box and with pagination.
中文：社区页有分类标签：全部、General、Find Buddy（约伴）、Lost & Found（失物招领）、Tutorials（教程）。点击标签即可筛选，并且可以和搜索框、翻页一起使用。

---

### Q: What can guests do without logging in?

**A:** Guests can view the live pool status, the lightning trend and radar, the community feed, and individual posts. Logging in with a verified account is required to submit pool reports, create posts, comment, like, save, send private messages, and use the assistant.
中文：未登录的游客可以查看泳池实时状态、闪电趋势与雷达、社区帖子列表和帖子详情。上报泳池状态、发帖、评论、点赞、收藏、私信和使用智能助手，需要登录并完成邮箱验证。

---

### Q: Can I send private messages to other users?

**A:** Yes. Verified users can open a user's profile or use the Messages page to chat one-on-one. Unread counts appear in the navigation bar.
中文：可以。已验证用户可以进入对方主页或消息页进行一对一私信，导航栏会显示未读数量。

---

## Assistant / 智能助手

---

### Q: What can the chatbot help with?

**A:** The assistant answers questions about live pool status, whether it is sensible to swim now, lightning and rainfall rules, opening hours, manual reports, community posts, and website usage. It requires login, replies in your language, and has a fair-use limit (a burst limit per minute and a daily message cap).
中文：助手可以回答泳池实时状态、现在适不适合游泳、雷雨规则、开放时间、手动上报、社区帖子和网站使用问题。需要登录后使用，会用你的语言回复，并设有每分钟和每日的使用上限。

---

### Q: How do I contact the website team?

**A:** The footer of every page lists the project's open-source GitHub repository and contact channels (WeChat and Gmail). Feedback and issue reports are welcome there.
中文：每个页面底部都有开源 GitHub 仓库和联系方式（微信、Gmail），欢迎反馈问题。
