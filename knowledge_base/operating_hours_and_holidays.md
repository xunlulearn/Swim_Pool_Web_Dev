# NTU Pool Operating Hours & Public Holidays / 开放时间与公共假期

---

### Q: What are the pool opening hours on weekdays?

**A:** On weekdays (Monday to Friday), the pool opens at **07:00** and closes at **21:30** (Singapore time).
中文：工作日（周一至周五）泳池开放时间为早上 **7:00** 至晚上 **21:30**（新加坡时间）。

---

### Q: What are the opening hours on weekends and public holidays?

**A:** On Saturdays, Sundays, and Singapore public holidays, the pool opens at **08:00** and closes at **20:00** (Singapore time).
中文：周六、周日以及新加坡公共假期，泳池开放时间为早上 **8:00** 至晚上 **20:00**。

---

### Q: Why does the system show "Outside Operating Hours"?

**A:** Outside the daily operating window the status indicator always shows **CLOSED (RED)** regardless of weather. The message includes the schedule that applies that day (Weekday 07:00–21:30, or Weekend/Public Holiday 08:00–20:00). The status returns to weather-based logic when the pool reopens.
中文：在每日开放时段之外，无论天气如何，状态卡都会显示 **CLOSED（红色）**，并注明当天适用的时段。开放后状态恢复为按天气规则判断。

---

### Q: Which days follow the public holiday schedule in 2026?

**A:** In 2026 the weekend/holiday hours (08:00–20:00) apply on: 1 Jan (New Year's Day), 17–18 Feb (Chinese New Year), 21 Mar (Hari Raya Puasa), 3 Apr (Good Friday), 1 May (Labour Day), 27 May (Hari Raya Haji), 31 May and 1 Jun (Vesak Day + observed), 9–10 Aug (National Day + observed), 8–9 Nov (Deepavali + observed), and 25 Dec (Christmas Day).

---

### Q: Which days follow the public holiday schedule in 2027?

**A:** In 2027 the weekend/holiday hours (08:00–20:00) apply on: 1 Jan (New Year's Day), 6–8 Feb (Chinese New Year, including the observed Monday), 10 Mar (Hari Raya Puasa), 26 Mar (Good Friday), 1 May (Labour Day), 17 May (Hari Raya Haji), 20 May (Vesak Day), 9 Aug (National Day), 29 Oct (Deepavali), and 25 Dec (Christmas Day). Hari Raya dates are based on astronomical calculation and may shift upon official confirmation.

---

### Q: Is the schedule the same during university vacations?

**A:** The website currently applies the same weekday/weekend/holiday schedule all year round. For special closures (maintenance, events, semester breaks), follow on-site notices and lifeguard instructions — they always take priority over the website status.
中文：网站目前全年套用同一套工作日/周末/假期时刻表。如遇维护、活动或假期特殊安排，请以现场公告和救生员指示为准。
